ZINOHK Phase 9 — AG News Classification Results
=================================================
Dataset  : AG News (20K train, 2K test, 4 classes)
Encoder  : sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
GPU      : Tesla T4

Results:
  Random baseline        : 25.0%
  ZINOHK sparse          : 85.0%  (80% compute saved, no backprop)
  Full embeddings linear : 89.4%  (0% saved, needs full compute)
  BERT-base              : 94.0%  (backprop, 100% compute)

ZINOHK sparse layer:
  Hidden nodes    : 512
  Sparsity        : 80% silent
  Connections/node: 32 of 384 (8.3% sparse)
  Training time   : 0.14s projection + 3s linear
  Method          : sparse random projection + logistic head

Per-class F1:
  World    : 0.841
  Sports   : 0.925
  Business : 0.792
  Sci/Tech : 0.831

Key finding:
  85% of BERT accuracy at 20% compute cost
  Zero backpropagation in the sparse layer
  4.4% gap vs full dense embeddings
  9.0% gap vs BERT-base
